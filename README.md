Each project lives in [`projects/`](./projects) with an impact-first README:

| Project | Problem it tackles | Impact |
|---------|--------------------|--------|
| [Waste Land 2039](./projects/waste-land-2039) | In my zombie game the player outruns every zombie, so a horde can only catch someone by being on their escape route first. Can it learn where *this* player runs? | A small predictive model with scripted tactics learned to anticipate; reinforcement learning never did. The newest version did 12% more damage in a pre-registered test, and the failures are written up as carefully as the wins. |
| [LoveSeek](./projects/loveseek) | AI companion apps forget what you tell them and have no sense of time. One messaged me at 4 a.m. | 790 downloads of 8 builds in its QQ group. Characters keep a schedule and a memory, and chats are stored only on the phone. |
| [小莲 · XiaoLianBot](./projects/xiaolian-bot) | A QQ group's 30-day activity rule had to be enforced by hand | Admins approve a daily list with one reply instead of tracking activity themselves; members get a reminder first and can ask for leave. |
| [RL for KARDS](./projects/rl-for-kards) | Can a bot learn a card game with hidden hands just by playing itself? | A Transformer policy that beats a lookahead-search bot in 91.6% of 2,000 games and passes all four of its done criteria. |
